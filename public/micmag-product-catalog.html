1. Screeding Filler 20L

Ready-to-use professional grade acrylic screeding putty. Delivers a highly uniform, ultra-smooth base to eliminate hairline cracks and plaster imperfections before topcoat application.

High-performance water-borne primer sealer engineered to shield topcoats from masonry alkalinity. Ideal for fresh plaster and concrete blocks to prevent peeling.

Premium solvent-borne anti-corrosion primer for ferrous steel. Infused with superior synthetic alkyd binders to lock out coastal air humidity and sea-spray rust.

High-binding, deep-penetrating water base sealer designed to lock powdery masonry substrates and seal porous blockwork surfaces for higher paint yields.

Elite solvent-borne binder for highly powdery, degraded or chalky brickwork and aged weather-exposed facades. Consolidates crumbly renders to prevent peeling.

Low-odor, water-borne undercoat premium prep paint. Perfect for high-density public hallways, school classrooms, and luxury hotel rooms.
