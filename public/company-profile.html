About Us

MICMAG Homes & Fittings is a premier Nigerian interior finishing and building materials company focused on deliverable quality products, professional workmanship, and complete customer satisfaction.

MICMAG Materials Selection Center & Quality Finishing Suite

The company provides building materials, interior finishing products, and turnkey fit-out solutions for residential, commercial, and institutional projects across Nigeria. Our stated offerings include direct product supply, architectural procurement, professional installation, and end-to-end project management.

MICMAG serves a diverse client portfolio including homeowners, architects, property developers, building contractors, corporate organizations, hospitality operators, schools, religious organizations, and public institutions.

Strategic Corporate Positioning

A dependable, project-oriented partner that combines direct authentic product access with practical interior and finishing execution under one trusted roof.

Authentic Sourcing

Precision Execution
Experienced technical teams handling screeding, joinery, and installation.

Serving projects in Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, and emerging real estate corridors.

Our Story & Growth

MICMAG Homes & Fittings was established with a clear vision: to provide Nigerians with quality building materials and interior finishing solutions that combine beauty, functionality, and long-term durability.

Recognizing the widespread need for reliable products and professional installation services in the Nigerian building sector, the company was built to bridge the gap between quality products and practical on-site project execution.

Building Material Sourcing & Procurement
Precision Interior Fit-Out & Styling

Over time, MICMAG has expanded its scope beyond basic retail product supply into full turnkey interior solutions, procurement, custom cabinetry, joinery, and nationwide project technical supervision.

Evolution of Capabilities

• Phase 1: Authorized dealership & bulk supply of Sandtex paints & Caplux chemical prep systems.
• Phase 2: Introduction of premium sanitary ware, luxury bathroom fittings, and designer tiling.
• Phase 3: Launch of full-service interior design, custom joinery, and digital showroom visualizer tools.
